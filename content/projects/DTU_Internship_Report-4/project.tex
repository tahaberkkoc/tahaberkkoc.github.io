\begin{ProjectSummary}
Internship report from DTU Compute on Model Predictive Control for district heating
systems under uncertainty. The study moves from linear time-invariant models to bilinear and
stochastic formulations with chance constraints, state-input analysis and Kalman filtering,
and combines them in a stochastic bilinear MPC applied to a single-pipe district heating
system, evaluated in open and closed loop with Monte Carlo simulations.
\end{ProjectSummary}
