\chapter{Conclusion}
During this internship, a complete MPC framework was developed step by step from linear deterministic systems to stochastic bilinear systems with chance constraints. The work also incorporated state-input analysis and Kalman filtering for state estimation under noisy measurements. The resulting controller was applied to a district heating system and evaluated in both open-loop and closed-loop settings using Monte Carlo simulations. The final implementation combines nonlinear optimization, uncertainty handling, probabilistic constraints, and state estimation, providing a basis for further development of stochastic MPC methods for district heating applications
