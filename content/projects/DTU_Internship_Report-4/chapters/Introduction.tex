\chapter{Introduction}

District heating systems require effective control strategies to maintain desired temperature levels while dealing with nonlinear dynamics, disturbances, and uncertainty. Model Predictive Control provides a suitable framework for this purpose by predicting future system behavior and optimizing control actions over a finite horizon. The aim of this report was to progressively develop an MPC framework, starting from deterministic linear models and extending it to bilinear and stochastic systems with chance constraints. State estimation using Kalman filtering was also investigated. Finally, the developed methods were applied to a district heating pipe model to study their behavior in a more realistic control problem.
