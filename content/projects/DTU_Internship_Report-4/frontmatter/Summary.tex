\chapter{Summary}
This report summarizes the work carried out during the internship on the development of Model Predictive Control (MPC) methods for district heating systems under uncertainty. The study progresses from linear time-invariant models to bilinear and stochastic formulations, including chance constraints, state-input analysis, and Kalman filtering. These components are then combined in a stochastic bilinear MPC framework and applied to a single-pipe district heating system. Both open-loop and closed-loop implementations are evaluated using Monte Carlo simulations. The final framework considers uncertainty, state estimation, probabilistic constraints, and nonlinear optimization within a unified control approach.
