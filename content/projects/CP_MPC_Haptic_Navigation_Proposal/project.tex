\begin{ProjectSummary}
Navigation guidance through a haptic vest when a person cannot visually follow the route.
Human reactions to tactile cues are delayed and vary across individuals, so the method combines
a plan-conditioned LSTM predictor, conformal prediction regions and model predictive control to
choose guidance cues while accounting for motion uncertainty. Online recursive least squares and
linear quantile tracking adapt predictions and regions to the individual. Two interaction
strategies, Hybrid and Non Stop Align, are examined in simulation as preparation for human trials.
\end{ProjectSummary}
