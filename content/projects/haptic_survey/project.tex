\begin{ProjectSummary}
A wearable vibrotactile vest and ZED 2 stereo camera system that guides blind users through
indoor spaces. A 24-signal haptic library was evaluated with 18 participants (recognition
accuracy rose from 43.8\% to 86.0\% after a short training), and four path-tracking strategies
were designed and validated in 2D and 3D simulation.
\end{ProjectSummary}
