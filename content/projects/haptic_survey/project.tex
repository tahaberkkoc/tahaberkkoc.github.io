\begin{ProjectSummary}
A wearable vibrotactile vest and ZED 2 stereo camera system that guides blind users through
indoor spaces. A 24-signal haptic library was evaluated with 18 participants, and four path-tracking strategies
were designed and validated in 2D and 3D simulation.
This work is still in progress; a detailed write-up will be added once it is completed.
\end{ProjectSummary}
