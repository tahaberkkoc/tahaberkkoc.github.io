\begin{ProjectSummary}
Ongoing undergraduate research at DTU Compute, continuing the \href{project:dtu-internship-report}{summer internship work} on
stochastic Model Predictive Control for flexible operation of district heating systems.
The single-pipe district heating model is being extended and the MPC structure reformulated
to account for nonlinear thermal dynamics and operational requirements, building on the
stochastic nonlinear MPC formulations with chance constraints, uncertainty propagation and
Kalman-filter-based state estimation developed during the internship.
\end{ProjectSummary}
